## About SunNova Solar
SunNova Solar is a rooftop-solar installer serving homes and small businesses in Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Chandigarh, Lucknow, Pune and Ahmedabad. We have installed over 4,000 rooftop systems since 2018. We are an MNRE-empanelled vendor, so customers can get the government subsidy through us. Office hours are Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM.

- 3 kW system: about Rs 1,85,000 before subsidy. Suits a monthly bill of Rs 2,500 to 4,000. Most popular.
- 5 kW system: about Rs 2,90,000 before subsidy. Suits a monthly bill of Rs 4,000 to 7,000.

Prices include panels, inverter, mounting structure, wiring, net-meter application and installation. Final price is confirmed after a free site visit.

## Government subsidy (PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana)
Residential customers can get a central government subsidy under PM Surya Ghar: Rs 30,000 for 1 kW, Rs 60,000 for 2 kW, and a maximum of Rs 78,000 for 3 kW and above. Example: a 3 kW system costing Rs 1,85,000 costs about Rs 1,07,000 after subsidy. SunNova handles the full subsidy paperwork on the national portal. The subsidy is credited to the customer's bank account usually within 30 to 45 days after the net meter is installed. Commercial buildings are not eligible for this subsidy.
Keywords: subsidy, government, scheme, PM Surya Ghar, yojana, sarkari, chhoot, सब्सिडी, सरकारी, योजना, छूट

## Financing and EMI
We offer EMI through partner banks and NBFCs. Loans cover up to 80 percent of the system cost, for 12 to 60 months, at interest rates starting around 7 percent per year for residential solar under PM Surya Ghar linked loans. A 3 kW system after subsidy can cost roughly Rs 2,200 per month on a 5-year EMI, which is often less than the electricity bill it saves. Zero-cost EMI for 6 months is available on credit cards of selected banks.

## Savings and payback
A 3 kW system generates about 360 to 420 units per month in North and West India. With net metering, most homes cut their electricity bill by 70 to 90 percent. Typical payback period after subsidy is 3 to 4 years, and the system lasts over 25 years.

## Installation process and timeline
1. Free site visit and roof survey (30-45 minutes).
2. Design and final quotation within 2 days.
3. Installation in 1 to 2 days after material arrives (usually 7 to 10 days after booking).
4. Net-meter installation by the electricity board: 2 to 4 weeks.
Roof requirement: about 100 square feet of shadow-free roof per kW.

## FAQs
- Does solar work on cloudy days? Yes, at a lower output; net metering balances it over the month.
- Does it work during a power cut? Normal on-grid systems shut down during power cuts for safety. A hybrid system with battery (extra Rs 60,000 to 1,20,000) keeps power on.
- Can I install on a rented house? Only with the owner's written permission.
- Which panel brands do you use? Tier-1 brands such as Waaree, Adani and Tata Power Solar, mono-PERC or TOPCon panels.
